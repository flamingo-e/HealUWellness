Inside Our Technology

Our medical-grade panels combine red (660 nm) and near-infrared (850 nm) wavelengths to activate your mitochondria and stimulate circulation exactly where your body needs it.

The Science

Red light therapy works at the cellular level by stimulating cytochrome c oxidase, a key enzyme in your mitochondria. This process:

  • Boosts ATP production - Your cells create more energy for healing and regeneration
  • Reduces oxidative stress - Protects cells from damage and promotes longevity
  • Increases blood flow - Delivers oxygen and nutrients throughout your body
  • Modulates inflammation - Supports natural recovery without side effects

The dual-wavelength approach ensures both surface-level and deep tissue benefits, making it effective for everything from skin health to muscle recovery and mental wellness.
